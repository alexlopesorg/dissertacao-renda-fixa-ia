\begin{abstract}
This dissertation investigates fixed-income investment recommendations
generated with the support of language models for Brazilian retail investors,
restricted to Tesouro Direto, bank CDs, LCI and LCA. It measures two properties
of each recommendation: the suitability of the product to the investor's
declared profile, and the faithfulness of the natural-language explanation,
that is, the absence of claims that contradict the product sheet. Synthetic
profiles are submitted to a prototype recommender, and each explanation is
checked against the product sheet using a typology of intrinsic and extrinsic
hallucination. The study aims to identify the conditions under which a
generated explanation can be offered to investors without case-by-case human
review.
\end{abstract}
